Where filtered learner time went
Full-candidate target preparation: 10,935.4 s = 3.0376 h.
Full-candidate online twin-critic scoring: 7,344.6 s = 2.0402 h.
Remaining retained training, selection, optimizers and bookkeeping: 1.3029 h.
Targets plus scores therefore account for 79.58% of filtered learner time.

For 100 candidate rows, the implementation computes next-state targets for all
100, evaluates both online critics to score all 100, and only then trains the
roughly 8 selected rows. Selection does not eliminate the work needed to decide
whether a row is selected. TES targets include next-state actor and target
twin-critic forwards. Scoring adds an online twin-critic forward. Retained
critic training performs its own forward/backward afterward.

The filter retains 16 logical updates per cycle. It reduces rows within each
update, not the number of Adam steps, target updates, or gradient reductions.
The filtered historical run actually completed 0.62% more cycles/updates at
the same received-step budget due to its collection/valid-transition trajectory.

Evaluation is the largest remaining wall-time component
The metric performance/eval means online simulation/replay collection, NOT
scheduled policy evaluation. CARLA benchmark evaluation executes outside the
learner Profile, every two cycles, followed by a checkpoint save. Each evaluates
250 scenarios. Even when checkpoint_interval is null, evaluation still calls
save_evaluation_checkpoint(), which calls save_checkpoint().

An alternating-cycle uptime comparison gives about 38 seconds median extra
time for evaluation plus checkpoint. The approximately 14 h residual is
consistent with those repeated calls, but also includes logging, setup, final
save/close and sampled-timer error. It is NOT a direct isolated measurement of
evaluation or checkpoint writing. These costs do not scale with retained rows.

Timer caveat
Profile samples every fifth cycle, synchronizes CUDA, and multiplies sampled
durations by five. Its cumulative phase totals are estimates; wall times from
summary.json are actual whole-run durations. Nested performance/env is already
inside performance/eval and must not be added again. Likewise, learner child
phases sum to performance/train and must not be added on top of it.

Implementation findings
1. Full-candidate target and score work is required by this fresh TD-error
   selection rule. It is the dominant remaining learner cost, not a bug that
   can be removed by applying the retained mask earlier without changing the
   scoring/target contract.
2. TensorReplay.get_batch gathers all five fields even when target preparation
   or scoring needs only a subset. Filtered actor/critic paths independently
   compact selected indices and synchronize metadata. These are real
   optimization opportunities, but their entire removable cost was not
   isolated; they must not be credited with the total full-candidate cost.
3. Filtered TES logged Dynamo recompile_limit=8 warnings near epochs 60/64
   for DriveSACCritic.forward, with batch-size guards near 256 rows. The
   unfiltered console has no such warning. Installed Torch 2.7.1 uses RUN_ONLY
   for the affected frame: matching cached graphs remain usable; unmatched
   invocations stop receiving new graph specializations. This is not evidence
   that the entire learner became eager. Its runtime contribution is unmeasured.

Next optimization priorities (not implemented)
For faster development turnover, first measure/reduce benchmark and checkpoint
frequency: they dominate total wall time and do not require changing the SAC
loss, though fewer evaluations/checkpoints change monitoring/recovery cadence.
For learner-only speed, target/scoring forwards are the primary ceiling.
Field-specific replay gathers and avoiding duplicate compaction are candidates
that can preserve training semantics. Compile-cache behavior needs a dedicated
controlled test before assigning it a speedup. Caching/stale scores, scoring
fewer candidates, or merging retained rows across logical updates change the
algorithm/update schedule and require separate experiments.

Do not add nested spans or synchronization-wait counters again. Detailed
learner time is only 0.052 s above the plain filtered run here; its retention
and exact row counts match. Target forwards still covered all 4,096,000 global
candidate rows in every measured case. Actor/critic counts exactly matched

exited successfully. GPU allocations returned to 1 MiB/device after cleanup.

The 7.526 s measured target+score cost closely matches the historical sampled
7.501 s/cycle. Even free retained-row training would leave those forwards,
collection, evaluation and checkpointing. Selection and the optimizer step
calls themselves are small in this probe; they are not the main explanation.
Gradient synchronization is included inside update spans and was not separately
isolated. Compiler-limit warnings were not reproduced in these short probes;
the historical warning's contribution remains unquantified.
